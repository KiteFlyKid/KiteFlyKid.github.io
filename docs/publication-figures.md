# Publication figure sources

The site uses the papers’ original figures, not generated illustrations. Click a thumbnail to see the complete figure at full size. Files are in `assets/img/publication_preview/`; bibliography entries provide their filenames and accessible descriptions.

- **PreScam:** Figure 1, raw reports to structured scam conversations. https://arxiv.org/html/2605.12243v1/scamchat_eg.png
- **Agent+P:** Figure 1, comparison with existing UI agents. https://arxiv.org/html/2510.06042v1/ComparisonView.png
- **PsyScam:** Figure 1, psychological techniques in prevalent scams. https://arxiv.org/html/2505.15017v1/GoldExample.png
- **AdGPE:** Figure 5, overview of malware detection and promotion reasoning. Rendered from page 7 of the repository’s `assets/NDSS2025.pdf`, cropped to the figure at PDF coordinates `(84, 52, 536, 302)` and rendered at 220 dpi.

`_layouts/bib.liquid` retains the pinned al-folio bibliography renderer with a customized image column, accessible descriptions, and full-size figure links. The upstream license is in `docs/licenses/al-folio-core-LICENSE`.
